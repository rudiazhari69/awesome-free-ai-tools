# Awesome Free AI Tools

A curated directory of AI tools that are **genuinely free to use** — not free trials, not "free for 7 days", not invite-only. Every tool listed here has a real free tier you can sign up for and use today.

> **Why this list exists:** most "free AI tools" lists mix real free tiers with trials that expire. This one doesn't. If a tool's free plan disappears, it gets removed.

## Honest Notes

- **Free limits matter.** Where a limit is verified from the tool's official pricing or documentation, it is stated exactly (for example Otter.ai's 300 minutes/month and Rytr's 10,000 characters/month). Where a limit is not publicly verified, the table says "Free tier available" instead of inventing a number.
- **Free tiers change.** A tool can shrink or remove its free plan at any time. Always check the official pricing page before relying on a limit listed here.
- **"Free" means no payment required.** Tools that only offer a time-limited trial, require a card upfront for the "free" plan, or are invite-only are not listed.
- **No affiliate links.** Every link goes to the tool's official homepage. No referral codes, no tracking parameters.
- **Not listed on purpose:** Sora (discontinued/closed), paid-only tools, and student-only free tiers (for example GitHub Copilot's free tier for verified students is not a general free tier, so it is not listed).
